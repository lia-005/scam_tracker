NATIONAL MERIT SCHOLARSHIP SCHEME 2026-27
Ministry of Education, Government of India (SAMPLE NOTICE FOR DEMO ONLY)

About
The scheme supports meritorious students from economically weaker families to continue their studies after Class 12.

Benefits
- Rs. 12,000 per year, credited directly to the student's bank account
- Renewable every year subject to 50% marks in the previous exam

Eligibility
- Indian citizen studying in a recognised college
- Family income below Rs. 4,50,000 per year
- Minimum 80% marks in Class 12

Documents Required
- Aadhaar card
- Income certificate
- Class 12 marksheet
- Bank passbook copy

How to Apply
1. Register on the official portal https://scholarships.gov.in
2. Fill in the application form and upload documents
3. Submit and note down the application ID
4. Verify through the institute nodal officer

Important Dates
- Last date to apply: 30 November 2026
- Verification by institute: 15 December 2026

Fees
No fee is charged at any stage of application, verification or disbursement. Beware of anyone asking for money. Applications are free of cost.
